GenBot

An agentic RAG incident copilot. A small online shop runs with real traffic, metrics and logs. Faults are injected on purpose, and GenBot works out what broke by choosing its own tools: live Prometheus metrics, semantic log search, deploy history and a memory of past incidents. Everything below is recorded from real runs.

How it works

Evaluation

Each trial resets the service, injects one fault, waits two minutes for symptoms, then asks the same neutral question: "Something is wrong with ShopAPI right now. What's causing it?" The answer's Root cause line is graded automatically against the fault that was actually injected.

Investigation replays

Pick a scenario to see what GenBot did: the tools it chose, in order, what the health check showed at that moment, and its final answer, word for word.
